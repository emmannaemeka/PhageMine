# PhageMine v1.3.2

Bug-fix release.

## Fixed

- **Secondary-caller coordinates.** Pyrodigal and Pyrodigal-gv report one-based
  inclusive gene coordinates, the same convention as PHANOTATE and GFF3.
  Earlier releases added one to both ends, so every secondary gene model was
  shifted by one nucleotide. As a result, exact agreement between PHANOTATE and
  Pyrodigal-gv was never detected and all reconciled loci were flagged for
  review. On the ten-genome benchmark panel the corrected reconciliation finds
  705 loci with exact caller agreement and flags 249 of 954 loci for review
  (previously 952 of 952).
- Final CDS coordinates, protein sequences and functional annotations are
  unchanged: PHANOTATE remains the authoritative gene caller. Results from
  earlier versions keep valid gene models and products, but their
  reconciliation tables, structural confidence and review flags should be
  regenerated.

## Reproducibility note

PHANOTATE 1.6.7 silently excludes open reading frames that overlap tRNAs
predicted by tRNAscan-SE or ARAGORN when either tool is on `PATH`. Gene sets can
therefore differ between environments (for example, T4: 294 genes with
tRNAscan-SE, 297 without). The recommended Conda environment includes
tRNAscan-SE; record its presence when comparing runs.
