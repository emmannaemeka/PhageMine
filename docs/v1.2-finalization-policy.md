# PhageMine v1.2.0 finalization scope

For DNA genomes, PHANOTATE is the sole primary structural caller. Pyrodigal,
Prodigal-gv, and legacy Prodigal (when explicitly requested) remain available
for corroboration and conflict detection only. Their predictions are retained
in provenance and review outputs, but cannot vote, replace PHANOTATE boundaries,
or add caller-specific CDSs.

Input records are whitespace-normalized and accept IUPAC symbols. Providers
that require A/C/G/T/N receive a provider-specific copy with ambiguity and
isolated unsupported symbols converted to `N`; the original sequence, hashes,
replacement counts, record ID, and provider are recorded in structured audit
provenance. Empty, malformed, or non-nucleotide records fail with a controlled
validation error.

Review summaries expose four deterministic presentation tiers:

* `NO_REVIEW`: exact agreement or no material conflict;
* `LOW_PRIORITY_REVIEW`: same-strand shared-stop start-site variation;
* `MODERATE_REVIEW`: material boundary disagreement or caller-specific locus;
* `HIGH_PRIORITY_REVIEW`: strand, split/merge, complex, or contradictory
  evidence conflict.

The tiers are not confidence scores and do not replace detailed reconciliation,
adjudication, or evidence fields. `confidence_calibrated` remains `false`.

The structural comparison utility is evaluation-only:

```text
python evaluation/v1.2_finalization/compare_structural.py \
  evaluation/v1.2_validation/temporal_heldout \
  --output evaluation/v1.2_finalization/temporal_primary_comparison.tsv
```

Functional product-name accuracy was not independently validated in PhageMine
v1.2. Automated multi-caller reconciliation beyond the PHANOTATE-primary
policy is deferred to v1.3.
