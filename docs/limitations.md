# Limitations and interpretation

PhageMine outputs are computational, evidence-supported predictions. Pfam,
VOGDB, PHROGs, and Swiss-Prot matches are not experimental confirmation.
PMFDB `NO_EXTERNAL_MATCH` is not proof of biological novelty, and searches
capped with `--max-seqs 100` are not prevalence estimates. Do not infer a
function beyond the reference annotation or automatically propagate external
product names.

PhageMine GenBank checks are pre-submission checks and do not claim NCBI
acceptance. A nucleotide sequence change requires a new analysis. Metadata or
annotation corrections preserve the original computational result and
regenerate only a versioned submission package. Optional resources may be
unavailable; Core records that limitation and continues where supported.
