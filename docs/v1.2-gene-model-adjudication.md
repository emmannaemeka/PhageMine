# v1.2 observational gene-model adjudication

Reconciliation describes how candidate models from multiple callers overlap;
adjudication evaluates those candidates and may produce a recommendation. In
Step 5, recommendations are stored separately and never mutate the legacy
PHANOTATE final CDS set or its functional searches.

Each candidate translation receives model-specific evidence. Identical protein
translations are searched once and safely reused using a cache fingerprint that
includes protein SHA256, adapter identity, database provenance/version, and
parameters. Evidence availability distinguishes comparable searches, partial
availability, resource-unavailable states, executed zero-hit searches, and
cache reuse.

Evidence is discriminating only when strong supported records are unique to one
candidate; shared hits are non-discriminating. A database match supports
plausibility of a translation but does not prove expression or exact start/stop
codons. Caller agreement is evidence, not biological truth.

Exact multi-provider coordinates can receive an observational
`EXACT_CONSENSUS` recommendation. Alternate boundaries remain unresolved unless
model-specific evidence discriminates them. Caller-specific ORFs, including
short or overlapping ORFs, are retained with weak/strong evidence classes and
review flags rather than automatically deleted. Strand and split/merge conflicts
remain review-required. Confidence is rule-based and
`confidence_calibrated=false`; it is not a probability.

Legacy `adjudicate`, `alternative_models`, and `acquire_alternative_evidence`
outputs are unchanged. New model-specific evidence and decisions are additive.
