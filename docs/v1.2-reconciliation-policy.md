# v1.2 observational reconciliation policy

Step 4 groups candidate models from any number of providers without selecting
or averaging a final CDS. Models must share genome/segment and have a positive
overlap that is either at least 30 bp or at least 50% reciprocal overlap. The
rule is deliberately separate from the publication benchmark's 80% evaluation
threshold and was chosen as a transparent development default for connecting
substantially overlapping hypotheses.

The overlap graph is built deterministically after canonical sorting by segment,
coordinates, provider ID, raw identifier, and protein checksum. Connected
components form loci. This preserves all candidates while avoiding one-base
touches and weak short overlaps; adjacent non-overlapping genes remain separate.
The implementation uses an interval sweep within genome/segment groups to avoid
global quadratic comparisons; dense overlapping regions still retain every
qualifying relationship.

Within each locus, exact coordinate groups and independent start, stop, and
strand groups are retained. Classes distinguish exact concordance, common-stop
alternate-start, common-start alternate-stop, boundary disagreement,
caller-specific, strand disagreement, split, merged, and complex conflicts.
Opposite-strand overlaps remain in a review-required locus and are never merged
as equivalent models. Multiple models from one provider are counted separately
as candidates but do not increase provider count or voting weight.

Candidate and locus IDs are content-derived SHA256 identifiers, making output
stable under provider and model input reordering. No functional evidence or
majority rule is used, and no final model is selected in Step 4. The policy is
not tuned to any publication-benchmark genome or sentinel.
