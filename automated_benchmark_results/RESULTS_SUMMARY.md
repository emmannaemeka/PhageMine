# Automated benchmark results

This is the separate `AUTOMATED_EVIDENCE_ADJUDICATION` track, not human
adjudication. The blind freeze contains 689/689 units. The automated decision
counts are recorded in `adjudication_freeze_manifest.json`; the conservative
procedure left unresolved cases missing. The primary utility table therefore
reports an observed lower bound and a missing-case upper bound over the frozen
evaluable-reference denominator. These bounds are descriptive and should not be
read as a comparative accuracy claim.

The original human-review materials and frozen benchmark inputs are unchanged.
