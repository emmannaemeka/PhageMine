# PhageMine v1.2 benchmark limitations

1. Automated evidence adjudication is not independent human expert review.
2. 476/689 units (69.0856%) were UNRESOLVABLE, substantially limiting primary
   functional-utility inference.
3. No valid superiority confidence interval is reported from this incomplete
   automated adjudication.
4. The +0.103205 observed lower-bound difference is descriptive and must not be
   represented as proof that the +0.05 superiority criterion was satisfied.
5. Zero automated unsupported-specificity calls do not establish absence of
   unsupported-specificity errors.
6. The classic curated phage panel may overlap with databases used by the
   annotation tools, so it is not a fully independent novel-phage test.
7. Within-genome loci are not independent biological replicates; the primary
   unit remains the genome.
8. Eleven compound reference locations were excluded from functional matching
   under the frozen protocol.
9. The original human-adjudication workbook remains available for future
   independent validation; no automated decision should be relabelled as human.
